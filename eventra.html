03 — Looking Closer

The problem wasn't just booking. It was everything around it.

01

I designed for discovery first.

Before anyone can decide to attend something, they need to see it — so the experience starts with discovery, not a search bar.

02

I gave choice more context.

An event page only earns a decision if it answers the key questions first — what, where, when, and how much.

03

I gave plans a place to live.

Once something felt worth attending, it needed a home — so saving and revisiting became core, not an afterthought.

04 — Rethinking the Journey

11 — Looking Back

I started with an event app. I ended up thinking about everything around the event.

Designing Eventra made me look beyond the moment of booking. The experience starts much earlier — with discovery, continues through planning, and stays useful even after the ticket is booked.

"Good product design isn't always about adding more. Sometimes it's about making the whole journey feel connected."

Eventra taught me to design the moments between the screens, not just the screens themselves.
